Step 5

Simple diet + workout combo guide

Realistic, beginner-friendly nutrition tips — no extreme diets, just fuel for your workouts.

☀️ Morning routine
Start your day right
Drink a glass of water before anything else
5-minute light stretch or walk
Eat within 1 hour of waking up
Include protein: eggs, curd, or peanut butter
💡 A simple banana + peanut butter breakfast works great for most beginners.
⚡ Pre-workout (30–60 min before)
Fuel your session
Banana or a small bowl of oats
Avoid heavy meals — you'll feel sluggish
Stay hydrated (500ml water minimum)
Optional: black coffee for an energy boost
💡 Don't work out on a completely empty stomach — even a banana is enough.
🥗 Post-workout (within 30–60 min)
Recover & rebuild
Protein is key: eggs, chicken, dal, paneer
Include carbs: rice, roti, or sweet potato
Rehydrate with water or coconut water
Avoid junk food — your muscles need real nutrients
💡 This is the most important meal of your day. Don't skip it.
